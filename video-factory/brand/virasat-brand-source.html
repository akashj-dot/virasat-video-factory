Profile marks and transparent wordmarks for light and dark layouts.

Linked cover + profile

Same pairing used on social profiles and product shells: a wide cover band carries the transparent wordmark; the circular profile sits below, overlapping the cover so the two assets read as one unit. Place the wordmark in the top-right; keep the tagline centred in the safe area.

Simplify Tomorrow, Today!

How the assets map
  1. Cover (transparent wordmark) — top-right anchor; scales down on small widths. Use transparent_bg_cover_dark_mode.png on deep / photo covers.
  2. Tagline — centred in the cover; keep padding so text clears the wordmark (this sample uses a serif headline in brand cream).
  3. Profile — circular mark overlaps the bottom edge of the cover; ring matches the card / page background so it feels “locked” to the header.

On light parchment or white covers, swap to the light wordmark and light profile PNGs the same way.
